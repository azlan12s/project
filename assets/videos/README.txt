Homepage Spotlight Fandoms videos

Add muted, looping MP4s for slides without a configured YouTube embed. Use
H.264 video encoding for broad browser support and keep clips short.

Place the files in this folder with these exact names:

  spotlight/anime.mp4
  spotlight/gaming.mp4
  spotlight/k-pop.mp4
  spotlight/comics.mp4
  spotlight/manga.mp4
  spotlight/tv-shows.mp4
  spotlight/cosplay.mp4

The matching image remains as a poster and fallback until the video is added.
Only the active carousel slide plays; inactive videos pause. Videos are muted,
looped, and disabled when the visitor requests reduced motion.
